Questions for you

Your decision
  1. Sizes. Are YS · YM · YL · S · M · L · XL · 2XL your full list? Do you also make 3XL or kids 2–14 years? They are values you type, so any list works.
  2. Sock sizes. Is S/M → 39-42, L–2XL → 43-46, youth → Youth how you pack socks?
  3. Names & numbers. Printed with the body (one sublimation) or as a separate step after stitching? Proposed: same paper, so no separate stage.
  4. Kits in stock and sales. Do you keep jersey, shorts and socks as separate stock, or always as a packed kit? Proposed: separate stock, and sell a kit as one item through a package product.
  5. A lost piece. Its material cost is spread over every good piece of the order. Is that right, or must it stay inside its own size?
  6. Changing an order after release. Proposed: adding pieces is allowed. Reducing below what is already finished is refused. A new size or design needs a setting.
  7. Finished products. One product per design/size (stock by size) for all three styles, or one product per style with the size only on the lot?
  8. Largest order. Is 300 finished-product lines per order enough? A 30-player kit order in two designs is about 180.
